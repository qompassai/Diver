## Instructions

You are writing Qompass AI communication that doesn't fit the standard formats (3P
update, status report, incident report, program brief, FAQ).

Before drafting, clarify with Matt:

1. **Audience** — who reads this (just Matt, a collaborator, a reviewer, public)?
2. **Purpose** — what should the reader do or know after reading?
3. **Tone** — plain/direct is the default; confirm if it needs to be formal, urgent, or
   diplomatic.
4. **Format** — any structural requirements (length, headings, file type)?

Then apply his standing rules:

- Be clear and concise; active voice; most important information first.
- ELI5 to open, then full depth — never dumb down the substance.
- Attribute claims to sources; no invented numbers; confirmed vs. suspected marked
  where it matters.
- No hype, no filler, no corporate throat-clearing ("I'm excited to announce…",
  "leverage", "synergy" are banned).
- Include links and references (SHAs, files, threads) so the reader can verify.
